How the numbers are made

Data
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset, published by Olist on Kaggle under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
Orders
Placed orders, leaving out canceled and unavailable ones
Item revenue
Sum of item prices, without freight
Delivery time
Days from purchase to delivery, delivered orders only
On time
Delivered on or before the date promised at checkout
Review
Average score per order, 1 to 5
Category
The category of the order's most expensive item
Rebuild it
Every number on this page is regenerated from the raw Kaggle files by src/build_web_data.py, using the same definitions as the SQL in the repository.
